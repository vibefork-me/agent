Vibefork is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS asks before the first start.

1. Drag Vibefork to Applications.
2. Open Vibefork from Applications. macOS says it cannot verify Vibefork. Click "Done".
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to "Vibefork was blocked".
   Click "Open Anyway" and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
4. Open Vibefork again. macOS remembers the choice; later starts and updates do not ask.

Before macOS 15 (Sequoia): right-click Vibefork in Applications, choose "Open", then "Open".

Every file of a release is listed with its SHA-256 in SHA256SUMS.txt of the release.
